How to listen

  • Melody: the strip under the line. Higher note means brighter, warmer, or more covered, depending on your setting.
  • Left to right: the line moves west to east, and so does the sound.
  • Low drone: the whole map's average. It shifts when the map changes.
  • Compare: first map in your left ear, last map in your right, playing together.
Imagery from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System. Sound is generated in your browser from the colors of each map image, so readings are indexes of the map's colors, not calibrated science values. Find more layer IDs in the GIBS docs or Worldview.
